About Haranogiyeche

Haranogiyeche is a community-based lost & found platform for universities, organizations and local communities. It puts lost and found items in one place, protects people's privacy, and helps items reach their rightful owners.

How it works

  1. Register and join a community with its join code, or create your own.
  2. Post a lost or found item with a description and a photo.
  3. If you think an item is yours, send a request and answer the owner's questions.
  4. The owner reviews your answers and approves or rejects the request.
  5. After approval, you can exchange private messages. When the item is returned, the post is marked as returned.

Privacy and safety

  • Posts and members are visible only to members of a community.
  • Nobody can message you until you approve their request.
  • Verification answers are visible only to the item's owner and community moderators.
  • You can report suspicious posts to community admins.
